## What changed from Phase 2

Phase 2 proved a fast packed-INT4 small-M W1 microkernel but correctly rejected the complete v7 hybrid because full-model C4 remained roughly 4.5-5.1% below stable stock.

Phase 3 did not relabel that failure. Instead it reopened upstream research and backported the relevant vLLM #43389 RDNA INT4/W4A16 MoE repack/interleave path into the known ROCm10 runtime. Real Qwen AutoAWQ repack, qzeros, scales and sampled dequantization gates passed before serving tests.

## Untuned Phase 3 milestone

Three independent fresh candidate starts produced C4:

- `191.450567 tok/s`
- `189.645424 tok/s`
- `191.426697 tok/s`

Median: `191.426697 tok/s`, approximately `+20.8%` versus the established stable stock+queue1 control (`158.489996 tok/s`).

However, untuned C1 and ~6K-context decode were weaker than stock. The candidate therefore remained a C4 win rather than a universal serving promotion.

## Stability investigation

A later same-process observation once dropped to about `145.12 tok/s`. It was excluded from the canonical independent-start campaign rather than hidden.

A roughly 10-hour soak did not reproduce that extreme event. Eight C4 rounds ranged from `175.520296` to `189.760035 tok/s`, with a median of `178.180645 tok/s` and a stable output-hash vector.

Telemetry did not support thermal throttling, power collapse or sclk collapse as the cause of the anomalous 145 observation.

## Cold-start limitation

The first dedicated correctness request after each fresh S3 process start repeatedly incurred about `4.61-5.08 s` TTFT. Hot correctness requests normalized to roughly `49-54 ms`.

The freshly restored stock runtime showed a similar first-request cold effect before normalizing. The evidence therefore supports a cold/lazy compile/cache penalty rather than a candidate-only steady-state correctness regression.

This remains a real latency-sensitive deployment limitation and must not be hidden.

## Final claim boundary

Safe claim:

> On the tested Qwen3-Coder 30B AWQ workload, the selected experimental R9700 Phase 3 S3 configuration reproduced a conservative C4 median of about 188.6 tok/s across three independent fresh processes, about 19% above the established stable stock+queue1 control and about 13.9% above a freshly restored healthy-hot stock observation from the same closure campaign. C1 remained near parity and long-context remained within a few percent of healthy stock.

Do not claim:

- official AMD or upstream R9700 support
- first port in the world
- universal 19-21% acceleration
- a `1.477x` full-model speedup
- `193.948 tok/s` as the universal reproducible result
- production deployment already occurred
- cold first-request latency is solved
